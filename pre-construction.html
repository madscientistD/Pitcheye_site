FAQ

Pre-Construction FAQs

What is an orthomosaic?

An orthomosaic is a single, high-resolution map stitched from hundreds or thousands of overlapping drone photos and corrected so that distances and areas can be measured accurately, like a very detailed, current satellite image of your site.

When should I use drone LiDAR instead of photogrammetry?

Photogrammetry is excellent for open sites and visual detail. LiDAR is better when you need ground elevations under trees, brush or crops, because laser pulses can reach the ground through gaps in vegetation. Many projects use both.

How accurate are PitchEye's pre-construction surveys?

PitchEye uses RTK GPS positioning to deliver sub-centimeter accuracy on orthomosaic maps and 3D models, suitable for engineering and planning applications.
