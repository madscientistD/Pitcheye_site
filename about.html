About PitchEye

Aerial Intelligence, Built in Idaho

PitchEye is a drone photogrammetry and AI analytics company based in Meridian, Idaho. We turn aerial imagery into accurate maps, 3D models and insights that help organizations manage land and infrastructure more efficiently and sustainably.

Our Story

PitchEye started on Idaho's golf courses. Superintendents needed a better way to see turf stress and irrigation problems across hundreds of acres, and to use less water doing it. High-resolution aerial imagery combined with AI analysis gave them that view, and showed exactly where water and fertilizer were needed and where they were being wasted.

The same capabilities that map a fairway also inspect a cell tower, survey a power line corridor, track a construction site and document a commercial property before sale. Today PitchEye serves agriculture, telecom, utilities and commercial real estate across the full project lifecycle, from pre-construction through operation, maintenance and transactions.

Our Mission

We believe detailed aerial data should be practical for every organization that manages land or infrastructure, not just the largest. By automating more of the workflow and driving down costs, we aim to put precise, AI-driven insight into the daily work of groundskeepers, growers, field crews and asset managers, and to support greener practices such as targeted watering and fertilizing along the way.

How We Work

Every engagement is hands-on. Our team sets up survey control for each site using RTK (real-time kinematic) GPS, an advanced form of GPS positioning that enables survey-grade accuracy. Survey missions are planned in-house and flown by FAA-certified drone pilots, and the results are processed, analyzed and delivered through our secure cloud platform, with our team available to walk you through what the data shows.

What We Do

  • Drone-based aerial photogrammetry and orthomosaic mapping
  • LiDAR topography and 3D modeling
  • Multispectral, NDVI and thermal imaging
  • AI-powered asset and vegetation health analysis
  • Custom survey mission planning with RTK GPS
  • Comprehensive reporting, analytics and ongoing monitoring programs

FAQ

About PitchEye: Common Questions

Who is PitchEye?

PitchEye is a drone photogrammetry and AI analytics company based in Meridian, Idaho. It began by helping Idaho golf courses reduce water use and now provides aerial intelligence for agriculture, telecom, utilities and commercial real estate.

How does a PitchEye project work?

PitchEye sets up survey control for each site using RTK GPS, plans the flight mission, and has FAA-certified pilots fly it. The imagery is then processed into maps and 3D models and analyzed with AI, and results are delivered through PitchEye's secure cloud platform with support from the team.
