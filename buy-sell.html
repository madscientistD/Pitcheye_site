FAQ

Buy & Sell FAQs

What does a drone due diligence survey include?

A typical survey includes an orthomosaic map of the property, detailed imagery of roofs, structures and site features, and a summary of visible condition issues with locations, all completed in a single site visit.

How quickly can a due diligence survey be completed?

Field work is usually completed in one visit, and PitchEye can deliver actionable results within 24 hours of data collection, which helps buyers working against inspection deadlines.

Is this useful for sellers as well as buyers?

Yes. Sellers use pre-listing surveys to find and fix issues early and to give buyers clear, current documentation of the property.
