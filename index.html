FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is drone photogrammetry?

Drone photogrammetry is the process of taking many overlapping aerial photos with a drone and processing them into accurate, measurable maps (orthomosaics) and 3D models. PitchEye combines photogrammetry with LiDAR, multispectral and thermal sensors and AI analysis to turn those maps into decisions.

What industries does PitchEye serve?

PitchEye serves agriculture and golf courses, telecom, utilities and commercial real estate, with solutions across the full project lifecycle: pre-construction, construction, operation and maintenance, and buying and selling assets.

Where is PitchEye located?

PitchEye is based in Meridian, Idaho, in the Boise metro area, and flies projects across Idaho and the Pacific Northwest.

Are PitchEye's drone pilots certified?

Yes. Every PitchEye survey mission is planned in-house and flown by FAA-certified drone pilots, using RTK GPS for survey-grade positioning.

How fast will I get results?

PitchEye processes thousands of images into actionable data in hours, not weeks, and typically delivers insights within 24 hours of data collection.
