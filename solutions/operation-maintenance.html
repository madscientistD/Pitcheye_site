FAQ

Operation & Maintenance FAQs

What is predictive maintenance with drones?

Predictive maintenance uses repeated inspections and data analysis to spot early signs of wear, such as corrosion, cracking or heat, and estimate when an asset will need work. Drones make frequent inspections practical, and AI helps compare each inspection against the last.

How does AI image analysis help inspections?

AI models review thousands of images to flag likely defects such as cracks, leaks and vegetation encroachment, so inspectors can focus their time on confirming and prioritizing real issues.

Which assets can PitchEye monitor?

PitchEye monitors assets including cell towers, power lines, substations, pipeline corridors, commercial roofs, golf course irrigation systems and agricultural land.
