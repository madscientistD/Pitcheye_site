FAQ

Construction FAQs

How often should a construction site be flown?

Weekly or bi-weekly flights are common on active sites, with additional flights at major milestones. PitchEye sets the cadence around your schedule and reporting needs.

Can drones measure stockpile volumes?

Yes. Photogrammetry produces 3D surface models that are used to calculate stockpile and earthwork volumes, which is faster and safer than walking piles with survey equipment.

Who can see the progress data?

Owners, contractors and project managers can access maps and imagery through PitchEye's secure cloud platform, so everyone works from the same current record.
