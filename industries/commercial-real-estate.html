FAQ

Commercial Real Estate FAQs

Why use a drone for a commercial roof inspection?

A drone covers large, flat or steep commercial roofs quickly without putting an inspector on the roof. Visual and thermal imagery can show ponding, membrane damage, failed seams and possible moisture intrusion, and creates a dated record for maintenance and insurance.

How do drone surveys help with commercial property due diligence?

Before closing, a drone survey documents roof, parking, site and exterior conditions in one visit. Buyers can spot deferred maintenance that affects price, and sellers can address issues before they come up in negotiations.

Can PitchEye survey several properties in one engagement?

Yes. Portfolio surveys use a consistent method across properties, so owners and investors can compare conditions side by side and prioritize capital spending.
