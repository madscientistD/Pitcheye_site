FAQ

Telecom FAQs

Are drone tower inspections safer than climbing?

Yes. A drone captures the same visual detail of antennas, mounts and hardware while the crew stays on the ground, which removes the fall risk of routine inspection climbs. Climbs can then be reserved for repairs that truly require hands on the steel.

How much faster is a drone inspection?

PitchEye drone inspections reduce inspection time by 80% compared with traditional climb-based inspections, while capturing a complete photographic record of the structure.

What does a telecom tower inspection deliverable include?

Deliverables can include organized high-resolution imagery of each sector and component, a GPS-tagged equipment inventory, a 3D model of the structure, and comparisons with previous inspections to flag changes.

Can drone data support tower modifications and new equipment installs?

Yes. 3D models and measured imagery give engineering teams accurate information on existing equipment and available space before they plan modifications or new installs.
