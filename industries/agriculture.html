FAQ

Agriculture FAQs

What is NDVI and why does it matter for golf courses and farms?

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) compares how plants reflect near-infrared and red light. Healthy, actively growing vegetation reflects more near-infrared, so an NDVI map highlights stressed turf or crops, often before the stress is visible to the eye. Superintendents and growers use it to target water, fertilizer and scouting time.

Can drone mapping help reduce irrigation water use?

Yes. Thermal and multispectral maps show dry spots, overwatered areas, broken heads and leaks. Fixing those specific problems lets you adjust irrigation zone by zone instead of watering the whole property to cover the worst spot.

How often should a golf course or field be flown?

It depends on your goals. Many operations fly at key points in the growing season, such as spring green-up, peak summer stress and fall, while irrigation troubleshooting or renovation projects may call for more frequent flights. PitchEye will recommend a schedule during your demo.

What do I receive after a flight?

Typical deliverables include a high-resolution orthomosaic map, NDVI or multispectral health maps, a 3D terrain model when needed, and a written summary of the issues found with GPS locations, delivered through PitchEye's secure cloud platform.
