FAQ

Utilities FAQs

What can a drone inspection find on power lines?

High-resolution and thermal imagery can reveal damaged insulators, corrosion, loose or failing hardware, hot spots and vegetation growing too close to conductors. AI analysis helps sort and prioritize findings across many structures.

How does drone vegetation mapping reduce outage and wildfire risk?

Mapping vegetation along transmission and distribution corridors shows exactly where growth is encroaching on lines, so trimming crews can be sent to the highest-risk spans first instead of working on a fixed cycle.

Can PitchEye build an inventory of our utility assets?

Yes. Each survey produces GPS-located imagery of poles, towers and equipment that becomes a searchable digital inventory with condition notes, which you can update with repeat flights.

Is our infrastructure data kept secure?

Yes. PitchEye stores data in a secure cloud platform with bank-level encryption and compliance controls designed for sensitive infrastructure data.
